Our Mission

Every Muslim student in the Allen School should feel seen, valued, and supported.

MiCSE creates an inclusive space where students can build community, navigate the challenges of being part of a religious minority in computing, and thrive both academically and personally.

Membership, events, and all MiCSE programming are open to all Allen students, regardless of religion, background, or identity.
