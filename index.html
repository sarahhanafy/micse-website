Our Mission

Muslims in CSE is a student organization dedicated to fostering a supportive, inclusive community for Muslim students in the Allen School. We provide space for connection, mentorship, and shared identity as students navigate their academic and professional journeys.

What We Do

A few of the ways we support each other throughout the year.

👥

Community Building

Regular socials, study nights, and gatherings for Muslim students in CSE to connect and support one another.

🎓

Mentorship

Connecting underclassmen with upperclassmen and alumni for academic and career guidance.

🌘

Faith & Identity

Creating space to navigate faith alongside academic life, including prayer space coordination and Iftar events during Ramadan.

💼

Professional Development

Resume workshops, interview prep, and networking with Muslim professionals in tech.

🎮

Game Nights & Socials

Low-key hangouts throughout the quarter — games, food, and a break from problem sets.

🤝

Peer Support

A tight-knit group of people who get it — from tough exam weeks to navigating being Muslim in tech.
