Muslims in CSE

University of Washington

A student organization at the UW Paul G. Allen School of Computer Science & Engineering, building community and belonging for Muslim students in computing.

Join Our Community

Our Mission

The organization's mission centers on creating an inclusive space where students can build community, navigate the challenges of being part of a religious minority in computing, and thrive both academically and personally. Through community-building, mentorship, and shared programming, MiCSE works to ensure that all students in the Allen School feel seen, valued, and supported.

Membership, events, and all MiCSE programming are open to all UW students, regardless of religion, background, or identity.
