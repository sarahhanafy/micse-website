Our Mission

Muslims in CSE (MiCSE) is a registered student organization in the Paul G. Allen School of Computer Science & Engineering. MiCSE is dedicated to fostering community, belonging, and support around the experiences of Muslim students in computing. Membership, events, and all MiCSE programming are open to all UW students, regardless of religion, background, or identity.

The organization's mission centers on creating an inclusive space where students can build community, navigate the challenges of being part of a religious minority in computing, and thrive both academically and personally. Through community-building, mentorship, and shared programming, MiCSE works to ensure that all students in the Allen School feel seen, valued, and supported.

Our Values

Community, mentorship, faith, and professional growth — the same pillars that shape everything we run, from casual socials to career workshops.
